## 1. Goals from the Prior Review and Results

**Goal 1 — Complete and sustain CCRN certification; use it to raise unit competency in vasoactive and antiarrhythmic drip management.**

Met and exceeded. Certification was renewed in September 2025. Beyond the credential, Rosa rebuilt the night-shift drip titration reference set into a single laminated card that is now on every med room door, and she ran nine informal bedside teaching sessions on amiodarone and nicardipine titration between October and May. Six of those were documented in the unit education log; the other three I observed directly. When the pharmacy changed the heparin nomogram in February, she had night shift converted and competent within eleven days without a formal in-service, because there was no education line to run one.

**Goal 2 — Precept a minimum of three new graduates through the residency program.**

Exceeded. Rosa precepted five: two from the July 2025 cohort and three from the January 2026 cohort, including Sandeep Chaudhary. All five completed or remain on track in the residency. This is the highest preceptor load carried by any nurse on 4 West this year, and it was carried on nights, in a unit running an 18 percent RN vacancy rate.

**Goal 3 — Serve as charge nurse on a rotating basis and improve shift handoff timeliness.**

Met. Charge coverage was 96 of her 156 worked shifts. Median handoff completion moved from 0715 to 0658 on nights she ran, measured off the electronic handoff timestamp. On nights she did not run, the median did not move. That gap is worth naming: the improvement is hers, not the unit's, and it has not yet been institutionalized.

**Goal 4 — Support the unit's response to the October 2025 accreditation finding on medication reconciliation.**

Exceeded, and this became the largest piece of unassigned work she took on. When the finding placed 4 West on monthly audit, Rosa volunteered to build and run the night-shift portion of the audit. She designed the twenty-chart sampling worksheet the unit now uses, trained four auditors, and produced the night data every month from November through June without a single late submission. Unit admission-medication-reconciliation compliance moved from 78 percent in November to 91 percent in June, against a corrective action threshold of 90 percent. Her own personal compliance across audited charts was 97 percent (33 of 34). I want to be plain in writing that the unit cleared its threshold in June substantially because of work she did for free.

**Goal 5 — Advance to Clinical Ladder Level IV.**

Not achieved, and not primarily for reasons within her control. See Section 5.
